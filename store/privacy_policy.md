# Interval Privacy Policy

_Last updated September 7, 2026_

## In short

Interval keeps your flashcards, study history, and settings **on your
device only**. There is no account, no server, and nothing about your
decks or your study activity is sent anywhere.

- No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs of any kind.
- No account or sign-in — there's nothing to link your data to.
- Camera, photo, and microphone access are used only to attach media to
  your own cards, and only when you choose to.

## What Interval stores, and where

Everything you create in Interval — decks, cards, note types, tags,
review history, streaks, and app settings — is stored in a local
database on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third
party, because Interval has no backend server to send it to.

If you turn on automatic backups, or use manual export, those backup
files are written to your device's own storage (or wherever you choose
to save them). We never see them.

## Permissions Interval may ask for

| Permission | Why Interval asks |
|---|---|
| Photo library | To attach an image you pick to a flashcard. |
| Camera | To attach a photo you take to a flashcard. |
| Microphone | To record audio you attach to a flashcard. |
| Notifications | To show the daily study reminder, if you enable it. |

Each of these is optional, requested only at the moment you use the
related feature, and the resulting photo, recording, or reminder
schedule is stored locally with the rest of your data.

## Importing an Anki deck

When you import a `.apkg` file, Interval reads it directly on your
device and copies its cards and media into your local collection. The
file never leaves your device during import.

## Children's privacy

Interval doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including
children, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of
Interval adds an optional cloud sync feature — we'll update this page
and change the "last updated" date above before that feature ships.
